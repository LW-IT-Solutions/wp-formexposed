# formexposed – Contact form spam check

Checks whether your contact forms are protected against bots and spam, adds a free protection layer and connects hosted form protection.

- Free form check from formexposed.com: finds the contact forms and checks for CAPTCHA, honeypot, tokens, rate limits and time checks, without submitting anything
- Free protection layer on your own server for Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Elementor Pro, Fluent Forms, Formidable, Ninja Forms, comments and registration: trap field, signed fill time, invisible proof of work, rate limit, header-injection and mail-domain checks
- Stopped messages wait 14 days in a local quarantine and can be delivered with one click
- Optional hosted form protection (formconfirmed) with no silent pass: if the service fails, a message is delivered only after an independent check on the site
- Optional monitoring, a rescue net for enquiries whose e-mail could not be sent, a form settings check and a storage limit

Requires WordPress 6.2 and PHP 7.4. Details in [readme.txt](readme.txt).
